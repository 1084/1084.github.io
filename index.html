About

Who is behind this site.

John Hubert LLC is an independent software studio. We build small, focused applications, with a particular interest in tools for learning and in software that respects the people who use it: our apps work offline where they can, collect nothing they don't need, and never sell data.

Current work includes Vitamin Village, a nutrition game for children; VCA, group video calling with a shared workspace; and visual-math, a visualization-first approach to university mathematics. Everything here moves through the same stages, from concept to prototype to testing to live, and we say plainly which stage each project is at.

Questions, feedback, or a bug to report: support@johnhubert.llc. Our privacy policy covers this website and every app we publish.
